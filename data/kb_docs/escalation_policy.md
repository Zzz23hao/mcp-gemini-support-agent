# Escalation Policy

## Background
Support cases should be escalated when they exceed service desk authority, affect multiple users, or involve security risk.

## Symptoms
- The same issue affects multiple users.
- The service desk cannot complete required admin action.
- The issue has security or compliance impact.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Collect user, service, environment, and timestamp details.
2. Attach screenshots, logs, and error messages.
3. Classify severity based on impact and urgency.
4. Assign the ticket to the correct escalation group.
5. Add a clear summary of attempted troubleshooting.

## Common Errors
- Missing reproduction steps
- Incorrect assignment group
- No business impact documented

## Escalation
Escalate immediately for security incidents, production outages, or executive-impacting issues.
